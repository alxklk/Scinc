# Sample sources

All four recordings are public domain. Each file is an excerpt re-encoded to
48 kHz stereo 128 kbps MP3, the format `filter.cpp` expects, with loudness
normalised to about -20 LUFS.

| File | Recording | Performer | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `music.mp3` | Small Note Boogaloo (first 2:00), by Ben Patterson | Airmen of Note, United States Air Force Band | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Small_Note_Boogaloo_-_Airmen_of_Note_-_United_States_Air_Force_Band.mp3) | Public domain, US government work |
| `song.mp3` | America the Beautiful (complete), arr. Michael Davis | Singing Sergeants, United States Air Force Band | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:America_the_Beautiful_-_Singing_Sergeants_-_United_States_Air_Force_Band.mp3) | Public domain, US government work |
| `male.mp3` | Reginald, section 1, by Saki (2:00 from 0:40) | Peter Yearsley | [LibriVox](https://archive.org/details/reginald_2510_librivox) | Public domain |
| `female.mp3` | The Gift of the Magi, by O. Henry (2:00 from 0:40) | Betsie Bush | [LibriVox](https://archive.org/details/giftofmagi) | Public domain |
